Problem Statement Goal Statement Redesign the app with a focus on simplifying the user interface,  streamlining navigation,minimizing repetitive steps, and improving  the clarity and placement of features. The goal is to eliminate  distractions like intrusive ads, enhance the overall usability guided  by heuristic principles, and deliver a polished, intuitive, and seamless  experience that empowers users to achieve their goals with  confidence.  Users are experiencing frustration due to the app’s cluttered  interface, confusing navigation structure, repetitive user  actions, and poorly placed advertisements. Additionally, several  features feel incomplete or non-functional, leading to  decreased trust and a lack of engagement with the app. These  issues result in a disjointed user experience and hinder users  from accomplishing key tasks efficiently.

Metro Section Redesign User Flow In the redesigned metro app, the user begins by selecting a metro line from a clean, card-style interface. They are then prompted to choose their current station from a visually accessible list of large, tappable buttons. Upon selecting a station, the app displays available train timings in both directions using neatly formatted cards. Tapping on a direction reveals a clear timeline view of   the   route, with   each   station   and   its corresponding train time shown in a scrollable list. The user can also expand sections to track the train’s real-time location and view upcoming stops. The flow is intuitive, visually organized, and   designed   to   provide   quick   access   to essential metro information. Flowchart MSRTC Section Redesign User Flow The user enters the source and destination stations, then  taps on "Search Buses." A list of cities or stops appears for  selection. After choosing a route, the app displays all  available buses with timings, route info, bus type, and live  status. Users can also view schedules from specific bus  stops or check depot contact numbers for help.  The user begins on a   home screen featuring easily tappable options.  From there, they select MSRTC, which opens up three clear paths.  First, the user may enter a source and destination, followed by selecting a city from a  list. They are then shown route options, each presented as a tidy card with essential  bus details.  Alternatively, the user can search via a specific bus stop. This leads to a screen  displaying bus timings in both directions, helping users plan effectively. Selecting a  specific time reveals further route and stop details.  There is also a third option to search for depot contact information, enabling users to  get in touch with the relevant authorities for help or booking.  The overall experience is organized, efficient, and user-friendly, offering fast access to  MSRTC route details, timings, and contact support – all within a well-structured flow.
